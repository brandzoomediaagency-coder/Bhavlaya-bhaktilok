# Bhavlaya Content Presentation Guidelines

**Target:** Editorial Excellence, Sacred Authenticity, Multi-device Typography  
**Scripts:** Devanagari (Hindi/Sanskrit) & English Latin  

---

## 1. Typography & Readability Architecture

1. **Devanagari Font Hierarchy:**
   - Headings: Serif/Devanagari with appropriate line-height (`leading-relaxed` or `1.6`) to prevent conjunct clipping (matras and halant).
   - Body Shlokas: Padded text blocks with distinct stanza separation (`॥ १ ॥`, `॥ २ ॥`).
   - English Transliteration: Monospace/Sans transliteration beneath Sanskrit with diacritics where appropriate.
2. **Elderly Devotee Ergonomics:**
   - Client-side Font Size Scaling controls (`A-` / `A+` / `Reset`).
   - High-contrast mode compatibility.
   - Clean Print Stylesheet (`@media print`) stripping navigation and ads.

---

## 2. Content Detail Page Anatomy

Every sacred text (Aarti, Chalisa, Mantra, Stotra, Katha) follows a 14-point structure:
1. Breadcrumb navigation (`Home > Category > Deity > Item`).
2. Primary Heading with Devanagari & English Transliteration.
3. Quick AEO Executive Summary / Significance box.
4. Deity, Rashi, and Festival association badges.
5. Interactive Audio Recitation Player (with time-scrub and repeat).
6. Font size adjustment and Copy Shloka button.
7. Original Sacred Sanskrit / Hindi Text with stanza enumeration.
8. Word-by-word meaning and Hindi Bhavaarth.
9. Phalshruti (Spiritual benefit and traditional recitation rules).
10. Authentic Scriptural Source reference (e.g. *Valmiki Ramayana*, *Skanda Purana*, *Hanuman Bahuk*).
11. Author / Scholarly reviewer attribution.
12. Interactive Share & Bookmark tools.
13. Frequently Asked Questions (Accordion + FAQ Schema).
14. Contextual related hymns and recommended daily prayers.
